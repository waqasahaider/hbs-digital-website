Why H b&s Digital

H b&s Digital provides artificial-intelligence automation and consulting designed to help businesses adopt and integrate AI into their day-to-day operations. We specialise in finding the manual, repetitive and time-consuming work in a business and replacing or enhancing it with AI-driven workflows and custom-trained systems.

We work with SMEs, startups and corporate teams across the Emirates who want to improve efficiency, cut manual workload and scale through the practical application of AI — not through slide decks or theory, but through systems that are actually running by the time we're done.

We're based in the SHAMS free zone and work bilingually in Arabic and English, so what we build fits the way business is really done in the UAE.

Automation-first
We don't sell software licences — we build the operational backbone your team actually uses, and keep it running.

How we work

Four principles behind every build

We build for your tools

Automation lives inside the software you already use, wired into your stack — not another platform for your team to manage.

Systems only pay off if the people using them understand how to run them, so we get your team fluent before we hand over.

We stay after launch

Tools change and models improve. We keep everything monitored, fixed and getting better long after go-live.
