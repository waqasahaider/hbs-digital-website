Integrated AI Sales System

One connected system from first click to qualified conversation and sales-team handoff.

Sales teams lose momentum when the website, social channels, spreadsheets, inboxes and phone calls all operate separately. We connect them into one branded sales system: every lead enters your company-owned CRM, receives the right response and remains visible from first enquiry to closed deal.

New leads can be called by a bilingual AI voice agent, followed up through WhatsApp and email, and routed to the right salesperson. Telegram alerts keep the team informed about hot leads, booked meetings, requested callbacks and conversations that need a person.

What's included

  • Brand identity, website and landing pages
  • Social media automation and lead capture
  • Your company's own CRM and sales pipeline
  • Bilingual AI calling and qualification
  • Automated WhatsApp and email follow-up
  • Telegram notifications for the sales team
  • Human ownership, handoff and reporting
Who it's for

Sales teams managing leads across several channels who need faster response, cleaner follow-up and one reliable view of every opportunity.

Specially integrated for sales teams

Every lead source, every follow-up and every handoff — connected

Where it helps

Inbound reception

Answer routine calls, identify the reason for contact and route urgent or complex conversations to the right person.

Lead qualification

Respond while intent is high, ask your approved questions and place a clean, scored record into the CRM.

Bookings & reminders

Book appointments, confirm attendance, reschedule requests and recover missed bookings without phone tag.

Follow-up & reactivation

Run client-approved follow-up for warm leads, service updates, renewals and dormant-customer reactivation.

How a call runs

Natural conversation, controlled execution

01

Answer

The voice agent answers or starts an approved call using your number, greeting and language rules.

02

Understand

It listens, identifies intent and follows the relevant conversation path without forcing a rigid menu.

03

Act

It checks availability, captures details, books, updates the CRM or triggers the agreed next step.

04

Handoff

It transfers or alerts a person when confidence is low, the caller asks, or the situation needs judgement.

Designed with human control

Every deployment uses client-approved purposes, scripts, calling windows, escalation rules and contact lists. Sensitive or uncertain conversations are handed to a person, and important outcomes remain visible for review.
