AI Calling & Voice Agents

Bilingual AI voice agents that answer, qualify, book and follow up.

When calls go unanswered, leads cool off and customers call someone else. Our AI voice agents handle routine inbound and approved outbound conversations in Arabic and English — naturally, consistently and around the clock.

The agent can answer common questions, qualify a lead, book or change an appointment, confirm details and write the outcome back to your CRM. When a caller needs judgement or asks for a person, it transfers or schedules a human follow-up with the full conversation context attached.

What's included

  • Inbound and approved outbound call flows
  • Arabic and English conversations
  • Lead qualification and routing
  • Booking, reminders and confirmations
  • CRM notes, outcomes and next actions
  • Human transfer and callback handoff
  • Approved scripts, rules and call controls
Who it's for

Businesses with missed calls, repetitive phone enquiries, booking volume or follow-up work their team cannot cover consistently.

Where it helps

One voice layer across the customer journey

Start with one tightly defined call type, prove the workflow, then expand only where the customer experience stays strong.

Inbound reception

Answer routine calls, identify the reason for contact and route urgent or complex conversations to the right person.

Lead qualification

Respond while intent is high, ask your approved questions and place a clean, scored record into the CRM.

Bookings & reminders

Book appointments, confirm attendance, reschedule requests and recover missed bookings without phone tag.

Follow-up & reactivation

Run client-approved follow-up for warm leads, service updates, renewals and dormant-customer reactivation.

How a call runs

Natural conversation, controlled execution

01

Answer

The voice agent answers or starts an approved call using your number, greeting and language rules.

02

Understand

It listens, identifies intent and follows the relevant conversation path without forcing a rigid menu.

03

Act

It checks availability, captures details, books, updates the CRM or triggers the agreed next step.

04

Handoff

It transfers or alerts a person when confidence is low, the caller asks, or the situation needs judgement.

Designed with human control

Every deployment uses client-approved purposes, scripts, calling windows, escalation rules and contact lists. Sensitive or uncertain conversations are handed to a person, and important outcomes remain visible for review.
